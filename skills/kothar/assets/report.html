02 · The ideaThe mountain that answers

Echo was a mountain nymph: only her voice remained among the rocks.

The symbol is her mountain: two peaks with a cave and, inside, an arch that repeats the outer one. It is the voice that comes back, as Eco gives back in your text the words people search for.

— The myth without the literal: no nymph, no speech bubble, only the place where the echo lives.

— Construction: two concentric arches, flanks at the slope later reused in the lettering.

— At 16 px the two peaks and the cave remain.
